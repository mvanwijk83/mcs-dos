*** BEEP ***
Emits a SID beep.

BEEP [duration]

Duration is in milliseconds. The
default value when omitted is 200 ms.


*** CHKDSK ***
Checks a disk and returns a status
report.

CHKDSK [drive:]

Type CHKDSK without parameter to check
the current disk.


*** CLS ***
Clears the screen.


*** COLOR ***
Sets text, background and border colors
for the command shell, or a preset
scheme.

COLOR /PRESET:n
COLOR [/FORE:n /BACK:n /BORDER:n]

  /PRESET  One of 5 predefined themes:
           1  PC Classic 1
           2  PC Classic 2
           3  High-contrast
           4  Terminal green
           5  BASIC blue
  /FORE    The foreground (text) color.
  /BACK    The background color.
  /BORDER  The border color.

Colors:
<preserve existing spectrum>


*** COPY ***
Copies a file to another location.

COPY source destination

source
  The file to be copied.
destination
  The drive and/or filename to copy to.


*** DEL ***
Deletes files (wildcards * and ?).
DEL * deletes all unlocked files.

DEL [drive:]filename
DELETE [drive:]filename
ERASE [drive:]filename


*** DIR ***
Displays a list of files on a disk.

DIR [drive:][pattern] [/B /L /O /W]

  [drive:][pattern]
      Specifies drive and optional
      wildcards (* or ?).
  /B  Uses bare format (names only).
  /L  Uses lower-case.
  /O  Lists files in alphabetic order.
  /W  Uses wide list format.


*** DISKCOPY ***
Copies the contents of one disk to
another.

DISKCOPY source: destination:
  source:       Drive to copy from.
  destination:  Drive to copy to.


*** DISKID ***
Changes the drive's two-character ID.

DISKID [drive:] [id]

ID must be two letters or digits. Omit
the ID to be prompted.


*** ECHO ***
Displays messages, or turns command-
echoing on or off.

  ECHO [ON | OFF]
  ECHO [message]

Type ECHO without parameters to display
the current echo setting.


*** EDIT ***
Edits one screen of text.

  EDIT [filename]

Supported edit keys are cursor keys,
INS/DEL and RETURN. RUN/STOP exits and
prompts to save.

Omit filename to be prompted at save.
To create a batch file, include a .BAT
extension.


*** EXIT ***
Quits the command shell and returns to
Commodore BASIC.


*** FORMAT ***
Formats a disk.

  FORMAT drive:


*** HELP ***
Displays help on MCS-DOS commands.

  HELP [command]

[command]
  Optionally specifies a command to
  view help about.

Command /? also displays help. If no
command is specified, a list of valid
commands is displayed.


*** LABEL ***
Creates, changes, or deletes the volume
label of a disk.

LABEL [drive:][label]


*** MEM ***
Displays the amount of used and free
memory in your system.


*** MOVE ***
Copies the source file to the specified
destination, then deletes the source.

MOVE source destination


*** PAUSE ***
Suspends processing of a batch program
and waits for a key to resume.


*** PRINT ***
Sends the contents of a file to a
connected printer (default device 4).

PRINT filename [4:|5:|LPT1|LPT2]

4: and LPT1 select device 4.
5: and LPT2 select device 5.


*** REM ***
Records comments in a batch file.

REM [comment]


*** REN ***
Renames a file on the same disk.

REN filename1 filename2
RENAME filename1 filename2


*** RUN ***
Executes a program.

RUN filename [/A address]

  /A  Load and jump at the decimal
      address.

Control returns to the shell after a
batch program is completed. Running an
executable file (PRG) ends the MCS-DOS
session; it will exit to BASIC.


*** TYPE ***
Displays the contents of a file.

TYPE filename

If the contents of the file does not
fit the usable display space, it is
paginated.


*** VER ***
Displays the MCS-DOS version and
copyright information.

VER
VERSION


*** VOL ***
Displays the volume label (disk name)
and disk ID, if they exist.

*** REBOOT ***
Reboots the environment.
